Our Heritage

The Story Behind Jewels by J

Jewels by J was founded in 2024 by Jerrilyn Mensah in Accra, Ghana. Starting as a creative passion for distinctive, meaningful accessories, it has quickly flourished into a beloved boutique jewelry house known for custom cursive nameplates and heirloom presentation.

I believe jewelry is not just an adornment, but a wearable memoir. From initial sketch to the final polish, each item receives individual artistry to ensure its brilliance lasts for generations. Every piece is thoughtfully brought to life with attention to detail, capturing moments, emotions, and personal stories meant to be worn and cherished every day.
